ProfStops

Current case study·Archive home

     Equipped with a MarcelDex and QR Scanner, session participants discovered their teams through the PokéScanner's virtual reality technology, then joined up in diverse parties to locate the three hidden ProfStops and rescue nine Pokémon from the clutches of their Team Rocket kidnappers! Each successful rescue not only earned a Gamification Badge for the party, but netted points for each member's team as well! Collaboration and competition, all in one!

     Thankfully, you can now teleport directly to a digital ProfStop. No need to travel to Broward College! Furthermore, after much hard work, Prof Trent has finally enabled the PokéScanner's web compatibility! Your only problem now is that you'll have to face Team Rocket without a team...
