ProfStop 3: Team Instinct

Current case study·Archive home

Team Rocket has captured Mr. Mime! He seems to be trying to communicate with you via gestures...

To save the poor Pokémon and earn 3 points for each team...

1. Devise an escape plan and communicate it through gestures! Record a quick video or take a short series of pictures of your group acting it out!

2. Share your video or photos with Prof Trent by emailing your work to thoy@broward.edu!

(Optional) If you’d like, share your video or photos on social media!

  • For Twitter, tag @TrentHoy and use the hashtag #BrowardLEAD.
  • For Instagram, tag @ProfTrent and use the hashtags #BrowardLEAD and #ProfTrent. (If your account is private, then send it as a direct message.)

3. When you are finished, click here!
