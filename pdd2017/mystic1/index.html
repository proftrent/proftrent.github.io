ProfStop 1: Team Mystic

Current case study·Archive home

Jigglypuff was on her way to sing at Bailey Hall when Team Rocket captured her!

To save the poor Pokémon and earn 3 points for each team...

1. Choose a song and sing part of it as a group! Use your phone's voice note function to record it! Don't worry if you can't sing that well. Jigglypuff can't really either...

2. Share your song with Prof Trent by emailing it to thoy@broward.edu!

3. When you are finished, click here!
