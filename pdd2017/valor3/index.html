ProfStop 3: Team Valor

Current case study·Archive home

Team Rocket captured Charmander, but it ran away to find help!

To find the poor Pokémon and earn 10 points for Team Valor…

With Cardboard Headset

1. Re-open this page in your mobile browser by clicking the  icon at the top of the app. The QR reader isn’t compatible with Cardboard Camera links.

2. Once in the new window, access the PokéScanner and click Save to App in the bottom right to open the image in Cardboard Camera.

3. Click on the image, then the red button to begin projecting the VR panorama. Place your phone in the cardboard headset and secure it with the rubber band.

4. Face the ProfStop sign, and find where Charmander went!

Without Cardboard Headset

1. Access this browser-friendly PokéScanner to find where Charmander went!
