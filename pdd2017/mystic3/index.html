ProfStop 3: Team Mystic

Current case study·Archive home

Team Rocket has captured Alakazam! His psychic powers weren't enough to save him from two henchmen!

To save the poor Pokémon...

1. Let him see into your mind and harness your mental capacities! Take the Bartle Test of Gamer Psychology and find out what type of player you are. Your fellow party members are welcome to take it too! Each player to complete the quiz will earn 10 points for their team! 2. Take a screenshot of your results and email it to Prof Trent at thoy@broward.edu! Don't forget to include your Name and Team! 3. When you're finished, click here!
