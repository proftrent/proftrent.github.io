French Vowel Explorer

French Vowel Explorer

At a glance

Context
Independent learning prototype · 2017
Role
Interaction designer · Developer
Audience
Learners of French pronunciation
Format
Interactive audio-visual articulation explorer

Need

Make the abstract relationships among tongue position, lip shape, sound, and IPA symbols easier to perceive and understand.

Solution

Learners manipulate sliders and select positions to hear sixteen French vowels while seeing the corresponding articulation and symbols.

Outcome

The functional prototype turned a static phonetics chart into an explorable model that learners could see, hear, and manipulate.

Why it matters

It is a compact example of how technical fluency can make a difficult mental model tangible without adding unnecessary complexity.

Methods & tools: Articulate Storyline · Rise · Interaction design · Audio
