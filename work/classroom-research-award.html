APPrendre le français

Classroom Research Award

Overview

APPrendre le français was a classroom research study examining whether educational apps could increase student success and engagement in beginning French. I integrated Duolingo into activities in two FRE1120 sections and examined students’ attitudes and course outcomes. The project reflects my interest in evaluating educational technology through evidence rather than novelty alone.

At a glance

Context
Adjunct ProfessorBroward College2014–2015
Audience
Beginning French students

Need

Understand whether educational apps could increase student success and engagement in beginning French.

Solution

A classroom study examining the integration of Duolingo into activities in two FRE1120 sections.

Deliverable

The project documented learner attitudes and course outcomes through a formal proposal and results evaluation.

Significance

It reflects my interest in testing educational technology against evidence rather than novelty alone.
