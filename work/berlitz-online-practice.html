Berlitz English 9–10: Online Practice

Berlitz English 9–10: Online Practice

Overview

As part of our English 9-10 courses (CEFR C1/C2), we decided to reimagine our online practice activities, asking learners to move beyond the "what" of language to explore the "how" and "why."

I led all aspects of the design and development of this project, including the initial brainstorming, management of creative writers, script and question revision, and multimedia production.

At a glance

Context
Learning Experience DesignerBerlitz2021–2022
Audience
Global English learners · CEFR C1/C2

Need

Help advanced learners move beyond the “what” of language to explore how meaning, nuance, and culture work.

Solution

A model pairing “fauxthentic” media with inference-rich questions and a personalized, real-world call to action.

Outcome

The production brought together 40+ actors from 18 countries and approximately 30 original videos for the reimagined courses.

Why it matters

It demonstrates my ability to carry a learning model from initial concept through writing, global production, and finished media.

Methods & tools: Curriculum design · Script development · Audio and video production · Global coordination

The production

I led brainstorming, managed creative writers, revised scripts and questions, coordinated remote filming, and completed the audio and video editing.
