ACT 4210: Auditing I

ACT 4210: Auditing I

How do you make a VR simulation feel like an integral part of a course and not just a shiny technology add-on?

Linda Kidwell’s course already had a strong instructional foundation. A Quality Matters review identified focused improvements, but the introduction of a VR warehouse opened the door to something more ambitious: reorganizing the experience around how students would prepare for, perform, and reflect on authentic audit work. The result paired traditional coursework with cases, Tableau analysis, practical activities, and two complementary inventory-audit simulations—including Kitchen Cordon Bleu, the accessible Storyline experience I designed and developed.

AT A GLANCE

Context
Senior Instructional DesignerNova Southeastern University2020–2021
Audience
Undergraduate accounting students
Scale

120+ NSU students since launch

VR simulation licensed by another university

Collaborators

Linda Kidwell · Professor of Accounting

Foretell Reality · VR development partner

Need

Build on a strong course foundation while making alignment more visible and turning conceptual auditing knowledge into authentic professional performance.

Solution

A mapped course architecture combining cases, Tableau analysis, practical activities, and phased preparation for an inventory-audit capstone offered in VR and an accessible Storyline format.

Outcome

The revised course earned a passing Quality Matters review, gave all learners an equitable route to the audit experience, and established a VR simulation still in use and evolving years later.

Impact

Launch-survey respondents described the experience as realistic, professionally relevant, and helpful for understanding auditors’ work and feeling better prepared for an actual inventory audit.

Takeaway

Immersive technology works best when the course creates a deliberate runway into it. Alignment, phased practice, and an equitable alternative made the warehouse more than a memorable event: they connected the experience to professional performance while giving every learner a viable route to the same outcomes.

Methods & Tools: Quality Matters · Course mapping · Canvas · Tableau · VR · Articulate Storyline · Qualtrics · JAWS · NVDA
