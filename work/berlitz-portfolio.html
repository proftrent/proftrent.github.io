Berlitz Learning Products

Berlitz Learning Products

Overview

Since joining Berlitz in 2021, I have helped shape the strategy, design, and delivery of a global portfolio spanning digital, instructor-led, and AI-enabled learning. My role has grown from designing individual experiences to defining curriculum architecture, assessment, quality standards, tools, and workflows that support teams working at scale. The portfolio reflects both sides of my practice: building learning products and building the organizational capability needed to create and sustain them.

At a glance

Context
Berlitz2021–Present
Audience
Enterprise and consumer learners worldwide

Need

Modernize and expand a global portfolio while improving instructional consistency, production capacity, and the learner experience.

Solution

Define instructional strategy, curriculum architecture, assessment, and AI integration while building standards, tools, and workflows around the work.

Outcome

The work encompasses 71 launched products, 1,030+ learning units, and stewardship of more than 400 active courses.

Why it matters

The evolution of my practice from designing individual experiences to building the organizational capability that produces them.

Methods & tools: AI-enabled learning · Internal authoring tools · Workflow design · Distributed team leadership

What I built around it

Standards, workflows, internal authoring tools, training, and scalable processes that helped a distributed team and contributor network create consistent work.

What shipped

Thirty-six e-learning courses, sixteen instructor-led courses, and nineteen AI-delivered courses—alongside support for nearly 2,000 regional, legacy, and third-party materials.
