Peers

Peers

Overview

How can busy college students make meaningful connections? Peers is a mobile app with a unique take on a proven formula for connecting people. Playfully dubbed "Tinder, but for friends", it matches students at participating institutions based solely on interests and pastimes. Check out the video below for a quick overview!

At a glance

Context
Graduate StudentNew York University2017
Audience
Busy college students

Need

How might busy college students form meaningful connections around shared interests rather than appearance or dating?

Solution

A mobile concept—playfully described as “Tinder, but for friends”—matching students at participating institutions by interests and pastimes.

Outcome

The idea progressed from a defined user need through interaction and visual design to a working mobile prototype and explanatory video.

Why it matters

It is an early example of the user-centered process I still use: clarify the real human need before deciding what the technology should do.

Methods & tools: User-centered design · Mobile interaction design · Prototyping · Video
