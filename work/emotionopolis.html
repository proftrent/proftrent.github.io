Emotionopolis

Emotionopolis

At a glance

Context
Graduate learning-game project · 2017
Role
Co-designer with Yael Ezer
Audience
Learners developing emotional intelligence
Format
Choose-your-own-adventure game prototype

Need

Give learners a safe way to examine how choices affect emotional balance, self-regulation, empathy, and interpersonal relationships.

Solution

A mystery world in which every citizen has two emotional faces and every player decision produces a direct, visible consequence.

Outcome

The interactive demo established the narrative world, choice system, and core learning model for a larger emotional-intelligence experience.

Why it matters

It showed me how narrative systems can make invisible internal processes visible—and turn reflection into consequential practice.

Methods & tools: Narrative design · Branching choices · Learning-game mechanics · Prototyping

The world

Citizens have two faces: one expressing pleasant emotions and one expressing unpleasant emotions. Players investigate disruptions in the balance between them.
