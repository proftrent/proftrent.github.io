DM-GY 6043: Theories of Media and Technology

DM-GY 6043: Theories of Media and Technology

How do you make media theory feel alive for both a graduate seminar and a global MOOC?

This was my first course as instructional design lead—and a fairly ambitious way to stretch beyond my Educational Technologist title. We developed two versions simultaneously: a discussion-rich course for NYU's online MS in Integrated Digital Media and a self-directed edX course within its MicroMasters pathway. One shared intellectual vision had to work for different learners, platforms, feedback models, and levels of instructor presence without becoming a diluted compromise.

AT A GLANCE

Context
Educational Technologist (Instructional Design Lead)New York University & edX2019-2020
Audience
NYU graduate students and global MicroMasters learners
Scale

Approximately 3,500 learners across both versions

edX course offered from 2020-2023

Collaborators

Scott Fitzgerald · Professor of Global Network Arts

Winslow Cavanaugh · Educational Technologist

Joseph Lee · Student Video Production Assistant

Need

Turn a dense theory course into an active creative experience for two audiences with radically different opportunities for interaction and feedback.

Solution

Preserve one learning arc, from critical reading to research, synthesis, creation, and informed critique, while translating presence, interaction, and feedback differently for a live graduate seminar and a global, self-directed MOOC.

Deliverable

Two coherent, platform-specific courses built from a shared 14-module core combining Rise-based lessons, discussions, hands-on activities, and scaffolded creative projects.

Impact

The edX course brought graduate-level media theory to thousands of learners worldwide and contributed to a credit-bearing MicroMasters pathway into NYU’s Integrated Digital Media master’s program.

Takeaway

A strong learning vision can survive platform constraints, but scale changes the economics of presence. Thoughtful automation and peer review help, but they cannot make a deeply personal creative course effortless or infinitely sustainable.
