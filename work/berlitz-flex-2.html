Berlitz Flex 2.0

Berlitz Flex 2.0

How do you transform a struggling digital product when rebuilding the platform—and most of the content—is off the table?

Flex paired self-paced e-learning with one-on-one live coaching, but its original implementation buried strong pedagogy beneath long, repetitive lessons and an inflexible platform. I led a pragmatic redesign that reorganized what already existed, introduced richer practice, and equipped the content team to deliver the change with the absolute minimum developer assistance.

AT A GLANCE

Context
Manager, Learning ExperienceBerlitz2022–2023
Audience
Global adult English learners · Beginner to intermediate
Scope
English Levels 1–4 · 39,000+ Flex 2.0 users to date
My role
Learning strategy · Instructional design · Team enablement · Validation

Need

Improve a high-profile blended product whose 3–4 hour lesson rounds, 90+ activities, and repetition-heavy structure were undermining engagement.

Solution

A leaner lesson model that consolidated review, streamlined video presentation, varied practice, added higher-order tasks, and made underused templates do more.

Outcome

Internal testers unanimously preferred the redesign and completed the sample round 20% faster, while rating it more effective, engaging, and varied.

Impact

Flex 2.0 has reached more than 39,000 users. Post-launch data through 2026 showed a 28.5% average increase in completion rates and roughly 10% faster course completion.

Takeaway

Innovation under constraint is less about adding features than finding leverage: reorganizing the experience, repurposing available tools, and building new capabilities inside the team.

Methods & Tools: Learning experience strategy · Instructional design · A/B testing · Branching scenarios · Audio/video editing · Azure Voice Studio · Editorial guidelines
