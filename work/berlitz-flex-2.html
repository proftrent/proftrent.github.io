Berlitz Flex 2.0

Berlitz Flex 2.0

How do you transform a nascent digital product when completely rebuilding the platform and the content is off the table?

Berlitz Flex pairs self-paced e-learning with one-on-one live coaching, but its original implementation buried strong pedagogy beneath long, repetitive lessons and an inflexible platform. I led a pragmatic redesign that reorganized what already existed, introduced richer practice, and equipped the global learning products team to deliver the change with the absolute minimum developer assistance.

AT A GLANCE

Context
Manager, Learning ExperienceBerlitz2022–2023
Audience
Global A1–A2 English learners
Scope
Four core courses representing 160 content units
Reach
39k+ Flex "2.0" enrollments by the end of 2025

Need

Improve a high-profile blended product whose time-consuming lessons and repetition-heavy structure were undermining engagement and progression.

Solution

A leaner lesson model that consolidated review, streamlined video presentation, varied practice, added higher-order tasks, and creatively applied limited activity templates.

Deliverable

A repeatable redesign spanning four core courses and 160 content units, supported by updated editorial guidance and new team production capabilities.

Impact

The "2.0" version of Flex English 1-4 has reached more than 39,000 users. Post-launch data through 2026 showed a 28.5% average increase in completion rates and roughly 10% faster course completion.

Takeaway

Innovation under constraint is less about adding features than finding leverage: reorganizing the experience, repurposing available tools, and building new capabilities inside the team.

Methods & Tools: Learning experience strategy · Instructional design · A/B testing · Branching scenarios · Audio/video editing · Azure Voice Studio · Editorial guidelines
