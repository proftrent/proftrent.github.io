At a glance

Context

Audience
Undergraduate French learners
Format

Need

Turn a skeletal departmental syllabus into a complete online language course.

Solution

A structured curriculum with curated resources, original assessments, discussions, projects, and animated media.

Building the course end to end

Working from a skeletal departmental syllabus, I structured and curated the content, designed and wrote the assessments, and taught several sections of the course. I also created animated videos where existing materials were insufficient.
